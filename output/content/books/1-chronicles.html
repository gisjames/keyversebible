1 Chronicles

Book Summary Insight

1 Chronicles reviews Israel’s history with emphasis on David, worship, and God’s covenant promises. The book encourages God’s people to remain faithful in worship and trust His enduring purposes.

Chapter 1

1 Chronicles 1:1
Adam

Why This Verse

Opening of biblical genealogy

Chapter Insight

This chapter traces humanity’s genealogy from Adam to the nations. The key verse emphasizes God’s sovereign oversight of history and peoples.

Chapter 2

1 Chronicles 2:55
Families of the scribes

Why This Verse

Memorable genealogical note

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the genealogy of Judah. The key verse highlights the tribe through which God’s covenant promises would unfold.

Chapter 3

1 Chronicles 3:17
Jeconiah’s descendants

Why This Verse

Messianic genealogy importance

Chapter Insight

This chapter traces the royal line of David. The key verse emphasizes God’s preservation of the Davidic dynasty.

Chapter 4

1 Chronicles 4:10
Prayer of Jabez

Why This Verse

Most famous verse in Chronicles

Chapter Insight

This chapter continues genealogies and includes the prayer of Jabez. The key verse highlights dependence upon God’s blessing and guidance.

Chapter 5

1 Chronicles 5:20
They cried to God in battle

Why This Verse

Victory through dependence

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the genealogies of the eastern tribes. The key verse emphasizes that victory comes through trust in God.

Chapter 6

1 Chronicles 6:31
David appointed singers

Why This Verse

Beginning of temple music emphasis

Chapter Insight

This chapter traces the lineage and duties of the Levites. The key verse highlights the importance of worship and service before God.

Chapter 7

1 Chronicles 7:23
He called his name Beriah

Why This Verse

Notable family account

Chapter Insight

This chapter records genealogies of several northern tribes. The key verse demonstrates God’s preservation of His covenant people.

Chapter 8

1 Chronicles 8:33
Saul begat Jonathan

Why This Verse

Links genealogy to kingdom history

Chapter Insight

This chapter focuses on the tribe of Benjamin. The key verse prepares for the introduction of Saul’s family line.

Chapter 9

1 Chronicles 9:22
Chosen gatekeepers

Why This Verse

Temple service emphasis

Chapter Insight

This chapter records those who returned from exile and served in Jerusalem. The key verse emphasizes restoration and renewed worship.

Chapter 10

1 Chronicles 10:13
Saul died for his transgression

Why This Verse

Divine explanation of Saul’s death

Chapter Insight

This chapter recounts Saul’s death. The key verse explains that Saul’s downfall resulted from unfaithfulness to God.

Chapter 11

1 Chronicles 11:9
David waxed greater and greater

Why This Verse

Summary of David’s rise

Chapter Insight

This chapter records David’s rise as king over all Israel. The key verse highlights God’s establishment of David according to His promise.

Chapter 12

1 Chronicles 12:32
Men who understood the times

Why This Verse

Frequently quoted leadership verse

Chapter Insight

This chapter lists those who supported David. The key verse emphasizes unity in advancing God’s purposes.

Chapter 13

1 Chronicles 13:10
Uzzah put forth his hand

Why This Verse

Reverence for God’s holiness

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the unsuccessful attempt to move the ark. The key verse highlights the importance of approaching God according to His instructions.

Chapter 14

1 Chronicles 14:11
God has broken through my enemies

Why This Verse

Baal-perazim

Chapter Insight

This chapter describes David’s victories and growing kingdom. The key verse emphasizes that God established David for the sake of His people.

Chapter 15

1 Chronicles 15:13
We sought Him not after the due order

Why This Verse

Lesson from Uzzah incident

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the proper transport of the ark. The key verse demonstrates worship carried out in obedience to God’s commands.

Chapter 16

1 Chronicles 16:11
Seek the LORD and His strength

Why This Verse

Widely quoted worship verse

Chapter Insight

This chapter celebrates the ark’s arrival in Jerusalem. The key verse calls God’s people to remember and proclaim His works.

Chapter 17

1 Chronicles 17:14
I will establish him forever

Why This Verse

Davidic covenant reaffirmed

Chapter Insight

This chapter records God’s covenant with David. The key verse promises an enduring kingdom and points to the coming Messiah.

Chapter 18

1 Chronicles 18:14
David executed judgment and justice

Why This Verse

Summary of righteous rule

Chapter Insight

This chapter summarizes David’s military victories. The key verse emphasizes that the Lord gave victory wherever David went.

Chapter 19

1 Chronicles 19:13
Be strong and let us behave ourselves valiantly

Why This Verse

Joab’s exhortation

Chapter Insight

This chapter records conflict with Ammon and Syria. The key verse highlights courage and trust in God’s sovereignty.

Chapter 20

1 Chronicles 20:8
These were born to the giant

Why This Verse

Closing victory summary

Chapter Insight

This chapter continues David’s military successes. The key verse demonstrates God’s faithfulness in securing victory.

Chapter 21

1 Chronicles 21:13
Let me fall into the hand of the LORD

Why This Verse

David’s repentance

Chapter Insight

This chapter recounts David’s census and repentance. The key verse emphasizes reliance upon God’s mercy rather than human strength.

Chapter 22

1 Chronicles 22:13
Be strong and of good courage

Why This Verse

David’s charge to Solomon

Chapter Insight

This chapter records David’s preparations for the Temple. The key verse highlights devotion to God’s future work.

Chapter 23

1 Chronicles 23:30
Praise and thank the LORD morning and evening

Why This Verse

Worship duty

Chapter Insight

This chapter organizes the Levites for Temple service. The key verse emphasizes orderly worship and faithful stewardship.

Chapter 24

1 Chronicles 24:19
Order of priestly service

Why This Verse

Organization of priesthood

Chapter Insight

This chapter arranges the priestly divisions. The key verse demonstrates God’s order in ministry and worship.

Chapter 25

1 Chronicles 25:7
Trained in singing unto the LORD

Why This Verse

Temple music ministry

Chapter Insight

This chapter organizes musicians for Temple worship. The key verse highlights the role of praise in serving God.

Chapter 26

1 Chronicles 26:20
Treasures of the house of God

Why This Verse

Stewardship responsibility

Chapter Insight

This chapter appoints gatekeepers and treasurers. The key verse emphasizes faithful administration of God’s work.

Chapter 27

1 Chronicles 27:1
Divisions serving month by month

Why This Verse

National organization

Chapter Insight

This chapter records military and governmental organization. The key verse demonstrates wise leadership and accountability.

Chapter 28

1 Chronicles 28:9
If thou seek Him, He will be found

Why This Verse

David’s charge to Solomon

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains David’s charge to Solomon. The key verse urges wholehearted devotion and obedience to God.

Chapter 29

1 Chronicles 29:11
Thine, O LORD, is the greatness

Why This Verse

One of Scripture’s great doxologies

Chapter Insight

This chapter records offerings for the Temple and David’s final prayer. The key verse acknowledges that all blessings and resources come from God.
