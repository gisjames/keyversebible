Jeremiah

Book Summary Insight

Jeremiah calls Judah to repentance while announcing both coming judgment and the promise of a New Covenant. The book teaches that God’s faithfulness endures despite His people’s rebellion.

Chapter 1

Jeremiah 1:5
Before I formed thee in the womb I knew thee

Why This Verse

One of Jeremiah’s most famous verses

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Jeremiah’s call as a prophet. The key verse emphasizes God’s sovereign purpose and commissioning of His servant.

Chapter 2

Jeremiah 2:13
Broken cisterns

Why This Verse

Powerful image of spiritual apostasy

Chapter Insight

This chapter confronts Judah’s spiritual adultery. The key verse highlights the tragedy of abandoning God, the source of living water.

Chapter 3

Jeremiah 3:15
Shepherds after Mine own heart

Why This Verse

Promise of godly leadership

Chapter Insight

This chapter calls the nation to repentance despite persistent unfaithfulness. The key verse reveals God’s willingness to restore the repentant.

Chapter 4

Jeremiah 4:14
Wash thine heart from wickedness

Why This Verse

Call to repentance

Chapter Insight

This chapter warns of coming judgment from the north. The key verse urges genuine repentance rather than superficial religion.

Chapter 5

Jeremiah 5:21
Hear now this, O foolish people

Why This Verse

Spiritual blindness

Chapter Insight

This chapter exposes Judah’s widespread corruption. The key verse demonstrates that judgment comes when truth and righteousness are rejected.

Chapter 6

Jeremiah 6:16
Ask for the old paths

Why This Verse

Frequently quoted call to faithfulness

Chapter Insight

This chapter warns of Jerusalem’s impending destruction. The key verse calls God’s people to seek the ancient paths of righteousness.

Chapter 7

Jeremiah 7:23
Obey My voice

Why This Verse

Covenant obedience

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains Jeremiah’s Temple Sermon. The key verse warns against trusting religious rituals while neglecting obedience.

Chapter 8

Jeremiah 8:22
Is there no balm in Gilead?

Why This Verse

One of Jeremiah’s most quoted questions

Chapter Insight

This chapter laments Judah’s refusal to repent. The key verse highlights the people’s rejection of God’s wisdom.

Chapter 9

Jeremiah 9:23
Let not the wise man glory in his wisdom

Why This Verse

True grounds for boasting

Chapter Insight

This chapter contrasts human boasting with knowing God. The key verse teaches that true glory is found in understanding and knowing the Lord.

Chapter 10

Jeremiah 10:23
The way of man is not in himself

Why This Verse

Dependence on God

Chapter Insight

This chapter contrasts the living God with idols. The key verse proclaims God’s uniqueness and sovereignty over creation.

Chapter 11

Jeremiah 11:8
They obeyed not, nor inclined their ear

Why This Verse

Covenant rebellion

Chapter Insight

This chapter addresses Judah’s violation of the covenant. The key verse emphasizes the consequences of covenant unfaithfulness.

Chapter 12

Jeremiah 12:5
If thou hast run with the footmen

Why This Verse

Perseverance under greater trials

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Jeremiah’s questions about God’s justice. The key verse encourages perseverance despite difficult circumstances.

Chapter 13

Jeremiah 13:23
Can the Ethiopian change his skin?

Why This Verse

Human inability to reform itself

Chapter Insight

This chapter uses symbolic actions to illustrate Judah’s pride. The key verse reveals that pride prevents people from honoring God.

Chapter 14

Jeremiah 14:9
Yet Thou, O LORD, art in the midst of us

Why This Verse

Appeal for mercy

Chapter Insight

This chapter records prayers during a devastating drought. The key verse emphasizes dependence upon God as the only true source of hope.

Chapter 15

Jeremiah 15:16
Thy words were found, and I did eat them

Why This Verse

Beloved verse on God’s Word

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains God’s response to Jeremiah’s complaints. The key verse calls for faithful endurance in ministry.

Chapter 16

Jeremiah 16:19
O LORD, my strength and my fortress

Why This Verse

Hope among the nations

Chapter Insight

This chapter portrays the severity of coming judgment. The key verse points beyond judgment to future restoration.

Jeremiah 17:9
The heart is deceitful above all things

Why This Verse

One of the most cited verses in Jeremiah

Chapter Insight

This chapter contrasts trusting in humanity with trusting in God. The key verse describes the blessing of placing confidence in the Lord.

Chapter 18

Jeremiah 18:6
As the clay is in the potter’s hand

Why This Verse

One of Jeremiah’s defining images

Chapter Insight

This chapter presents the potter and the clay. The key verse emphasizes God’s sovereign right to shape nations and individuals.

Chapter 19

Jeremiah 19:11
So will I break this people

Why This Verse

Symbolic judgment

Chapter Insight

This chapter uses a broken vessel to symbolize Judah’s coming destruction. The key verse illustrates the consequences of persistent rebellion.

Chapter 20

Jeremiah 20:9
His word was in mine heart as a burning fire

Why This Verse

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Jeremiah’s persecution and discouragement. The key verse reveals the irresistible power of God’s word within His servant.

Jeremiah 21:8
I set before you the way of life and death

Choice and accountability

Why This Verse

Dramatic prophetic appeal

Chapter Insight

This chapter addresses Judah’s kings and leaders. The key verse emphasizes justice, righteousness, and care for the vulnerable.

Chapter 23

Jeremiah 23:29
Is not My word like a fire?

Why This Verse

One of Jeremiah’s most quoted verses

Chapter Insight

This chapter condemns false shepherds and promises a righteous King. The key verse points to the coming Messiah, the righteous Branch.

Chapter 24

I will give them a heart to know Me

Why This Verse

Promise of restoration

Chapter Insight

This chapter uses the vision of good and bad figs. The key verse distinguishes those whom God will restore from those facing judgment.

Jeremiah 25:11
These nations shall serve Babylon seventy years

Why This Verse

Key exile prophecy

Chapter Insight

This chapter summarizes Jeremiah’s message and predicts seventy years of exile. The key verse demonstrates God’s sovereignty over nations and history.

Chapter 26

Jeremiah 26:13
Amend your ways and your doings

Why This Verse

Call to repentance

Chapter Insight

This chapter records opposition to Jeremiah’s preaching. The key verse highlights the courage required to proclaim God’s truth faithfully.

Jeremiah 27:5
I have made the earth

Why This Verse

God’s sovereign authority

Chapter Insight

This chapter uses a yoke to symbolize Babylon’s dominance. The key verse calls the nations to submit to God’s appointed discipline.

Chapter 28

Jeremiah 28:9
When the word of the prophet comes to pass

Why This Verse

Test of a true prophet

Chapter Insight

This chapter contrasts true and false prophecy through Hananiah. The key verse emphasizes that God’s word will ultimately prove true.

Chapter 29

Jeremiah 29:11
I know the plans I have for you

Why This Verse

One of the most quoted verses in the Bible

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains Jeremiah’s letter to the exiles. The key verse encourages trust in God’s future plans despite present hardship.

Chapter 30

Jeremiah 30:17
I will restore health unto thee

Why This Verse

Restoration promise

Chapter Insight

This chapter promises Israel’s future restoration. The key verse points to God’s covenant faithfulness beyond judgment.

Chapter 31

Jeremiah 31:3
I have loved thee with an everlasting love

Why This Verse

One of Jeremiah’s greatest promises

Chapter Insight

This chapter announces the New Covenant. The key verse promises God’s law written upon the heart.

Chapter 32

Jeremiah 32:17

Why This Verse

Famous declaration of faith

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Jeremiah’s purchase of a field during the siege. The key verse demonstrates faith in God’s promise of future restoration.

Chapter 33

Jeremiah 33:3
Call unto Me, and I will answer thee

Why This Verse

Widely quoted prayer promise

Chapter Insight

This chapter promises the restoration of Jerusalem and David’s line. The key verse reaffirms God’s covenant with David and His people.

Chapter 34

Jeremiah 34:15
You had done right in My sight

Why This Verse

Covenant accountability

Chapter Insight

This chapter condemns Judah’s failure to honor its covenant obligations. The key verse highlights the seriousness of breaking promises before God.

Chapter 35

Jeremiah 35:19
Jonadab shall not want a man to stand before Me

Why This Verse

Rechabite faithfulness rewarded

Chapter Insight

This chapter contrasts Judah’s disobedience with the faithfulness of the Rechabites. The key verse commends steadfast obedience.

Chapter 36

Jeremiah 36:23
The king cut it with a penknife

Why This Verse

Rejection of God’s word

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the burning of Jeremiah’s scroll. The key verse demonstrates that God’s word cannot be destroyed by human opposition.

Chapter 37

Jeremiah 37:17
Is there any word from the LORD?

Why This Verse

Zedekiah’s secret inquiry

Chapter Insight

This chapter recounts Jeremiah’s imprisonment during Jerusalem’s siege. The key verse highlights faithful proclamation despite rejection.

Chapter 38

Jeremiah 38:6
They cast Jeremiah into the dungeon

Why This Verse

One of Jeremiah’s most memorable events

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Jeremiah’s rescue from a cistern. The key verse emphasizes the cost of speaking God’s truth.

Chapter 39

Jeremiah 39:18
Because thou hast put thy trust in Me

Why This Verse

Deliverance through faith

Chapter Insight

This chapter describes Jerusalem’s fall. The key verse demonstrates the fulfillment of God’s warnings through the prophets.

Jeremiah 40:4
See, all the land is before thee

Why This Verse

Aftermath of Jerusalem’s fall

Chapter Insight

This chapter records events following Jerusalem’s destruction. The key verse reveals God’s continued care for a remnant.

Chapter 41

Jeremiah 41:17
They departed to go into Egypt

Why This Verse

Continued instability

Chapter Insight

This chapter recounts the assassination of Gedaliah. The key verse illustrates the tragic consequences of political violence and distrust.

Chapter 42

Jeremiah 42:6
Whether it be good or evil, we will obey

Why This Verse

Promise later broken

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the people’s request for guidance. The key verse highlights the importance of obeying God after seeking His will.

Chapter 43

Jeremiah 43:7
They obeyed not the voice of the LORD

Why This Verse

Rebellion continues

Chapter Insight

This chapter describes Judah’s disobedient flight to Egypt. The key verse demonstrates the danger of rejecting God’s clear direction.

Chapter 44

We will certainly do whatsoever thing goes forth out of our own mouth

Why This Verse

Defiant apostasy

Chapter Insight

This chapter rebukes continued idolatry in Egypt. The key verse reveals the persistence of rebellion despite judgment.

Jeremiah 45:5
Seekest thou great things for thyself? Seek them not

Famous personal admonition

Chapter Insight

This chapter offers encouragement to Baruch. The key verse teaches contentment and trust amid difficult times.

Chapter 46

Jeremiah 46:27
Fear not, O Jacob My servant

Why This Verse

Hope amid judgment

Chapter Insight

This chapter prophesies judgment against Egypt. The key verse demonstrates God’s sovereignty over powerful nations.

Chapter 47

Jeremiah 47:6
O thou sword of the LORD

Why This Verse

Memorable prophetic image

Chapter Insight

This chapter announces judgment upon the Philistines. The key verse emphasizes that no nation stands outside God’s authority.

Jeremiah 48:10
Cursed be he that doeth the work of the LORD deceitfully

Why This Verse

Frequently quoted warning

Chapter Insight

This chapter pronounces judgment against Moab. The key verse warns against pride and self-confidence.

Chapter 49

Jeremiah 49:16
The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee

Judgment on Edom

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains prophecies against several nations. The key verse demonstrates God’s rule over all peoples and kingdoms.

Jeremiah 50:6
My people have been lost sheep

This chapter foretells Babylon’s downfall. The key verse reveals God’s justice against oppressive empires.

The LORD hath brought forth our righteousness

Why This Verse

Hope of vindication

Chapter Insight

Jeremiah 52:31
Evil-merodach lifted up the head of Jehoiachin

Hopeful conclusion

Chapter Insight

This chapter recounts Jerusalem’s fall and the exile. The key verse concludes the book by affirming the historical fulfillment of God’s word while preserving hope through Jehoiachin’s release.
