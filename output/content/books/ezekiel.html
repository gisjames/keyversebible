Ezekiel

Book Summary Insight

Ezekiel proclaims God’s holiness, judgment, and future restoration through vivid visions and prophetic signs. The book teaches that God acts for the sake of His holy name and will restore His people.

Chapter 1

Ezekiel 1:28
The appearance of the likeness of the glory of the LORD

Why This Verse

Foundational vision of God’s glory

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Ezekiel’s vision of God’s glory. The key verse reveals God’s majesty and sovereignty even during exile.

Chapter 2

Ezekiel 2:6
Be not afraid of them

Why This Verse

Call to prophetic courage

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains Ezekiel’s commission as a prophet. The key verse emphasizes faithful proclamation regardless of the people’s response.

Chapter 3

Ezekiel 3:17
Son of man, I have made thee a watchman

Why This Verse

One of Ezekiel’s defining verses

Chapter Insight

This chapter establishes Ezekiel as a watchman for Israel. The key verse highlights personal responsibility to warn others of God’s truth.

Chapter 4

Ezekiel 4:6
I have appointed thee each day for a year

Why This Verse

Famous prophetic sign

Chapter Insight

This chapter uses symbolic acts to portray Jerusalem’s coming siege. The key verse demonstrates the certainty of God’s judgment.

Chapter 5

Ezekiel 5:8
I, even I, am against thee

Why This Verse

Judgment against Jerusalem

Chapter Insight

This chapter symbolizes the scattering of Jerusalem’s inhabitants. The key verse emphasizes the consequences of persistent rebellion.

Chapter 6

Ezekiel 6:10
They shall know that I am the LORD

Why This Verse

Repeated theme in Ezekiel

Chapter Insight

This chapter prophesies against Israel’s idolatrous high places. The key verse reveals God’s desire that His people recognize Him as Lord.

Chapter 7

Ezekiel 7:19
Their silver and gold shall not deliver them

Why This Verse

Futility of wealth

Chapter Insight

This chapter announces the approaching end of judgment. The key verse emphasizes the inevitability of God’s justice.

Chapter 8

Ezekiel 8:6
Great abominations

Why This Verse

Temple corruption exposed

Chapter Insight

This chapter reveals the hidden idolatry within the Temple. The key verse exposes the spiritual corruption that provoked God’s judgment.

Chapter 9

Ezekiel 9:4
Set a mark upon the foreheads

Why This Verse

Protection of the faithful remnant

Chapter Insight

This chapter depicts judgment upon Jerusalem with protection for the faithful remnant. The key verse highlights God’s distinction between the righteous and the wicked.

Chapter 10

Ezekiel 10:18
The glory of the LORD departed

Why This Verse

Major turning point

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the departure of God’s glory from the Temple. The key verse symbolizes the seriousness of covenant unfaithfulness.

Chapter 11

Ezekiel 11:19
I will give them one heart and a new spirit

Why This Verse

Major restoration promise

Chapter Insight

This chapter promises future restoration despite present judgment. The key verse foretells God giving His people a new heart and spirit.

Chapter 12

Ezekiel 12:25
I am the LORD; I will speak

Why This Verse

Certainty of God’s word

Chapter Insight

This chapter portrays exile through symbolic actions. The key verse demonstrates that God’s warnings will surely come to pass.

Chapter 13

Ezekiel 13:10
They have seduced My people, saying, Peace

Why This Verse

False prophets condemned

Chapter Insight

This chapter condemns false prophets and false assurances. The key verse emphasizes the danger of proclaiming peace where God has not spoken.

Chapter 14

Ezekiel 14:14
Though Noah, Daniel, and Job were in it

Why This Verse

Individual responsibility emphasized

Chapter Insight

This chapter addresses idolatry within the heart. The key verse teaches that true repentance must be inward as well as outward.

Chapter 15

Ezekiel 15:6
As the vine among the trees

Why This Verse

Worthless vine metaphor

Chapter Insight

This chapter compares Jerusalem to a useless vine. The key verse illustrates the consequences of failing God’s intended purpose.

Chapter 16

Ezekiel 16:60
I will remember My covenant with thee

Why This Verse

Grace despite unfaithfulness

Chapter Insight

This chapter portrays Jerusalem as an unfaithful spouse. The key verse highlights God’s grace despite profound covenant betrayal.

Chapter 17

Ezekiel 17:24
I the LORD bring down the high tree

Why This Verse

God’s sovereignty over nations

Chapter Insight

This chapter uses a parable of eagles and a vine. The key verse points to God’s ultimate establishment of His kingdom.

Chapter 18

Ezekiel 18:20
The soul that sinneth, it shall die

Why This Verse

One of Ezekiel’s most important theological statements

Chapter Insight

This chapter emphasizes individual responsibility before God. The key verse teaches that each person is accountable for his own choices.

Chapter 19

Ezekiel 19:10
Thy mother is like a vine

Why This Verse

Lament for Judah’s rulers

Chapter Insight

This chapter laments the downfall of Judah’s leaders. The key verse reflects the tragic consequences of failed leadership.

Chapter 20

Ezekiel 20:12
I gave them My Sabbaths

Why This Verse

Covenant sign emphasized

Chapter Insight

This chapter reviews Israel’s history of rebellion. The key verse highlights God’s faithfulness despite continual disobedience.

Chapter 21

Ezekiel 21:27
Until He come whose right it is

Why This Verse

Frequently viewed as messianic

Chapter Insight

This chapter announces the sword of God’s judgment. The key verse emphasizes the certainty of coming punishment.

Chapter 22

Ezekiel 22:30
I sought for a man among them

Why This Verse

One of the most quoted verses in Ezekiel

Chapter Insight

This chapter catalogs Jerusalem’s sins. The key verse reveals God’s search for someone to stand in the gap for the nation.

Chapter 23

Ezekiel 23:49
Ye shall know that I am the Lord GOD

Why This Verse

Judgment and recognition

Chapter Insight

This chapter uses two sisters to depict Israel’s and Judah’s unfaithfulness. The key verse demonstrates the seriousness of spiritual adultery.

Chapter 24

Ezekiel 24:16
I take away from thee the desire of thine eyes

Why This Verse

Profound prophetic sign

Chapter Insight

This chapter marks the beginning of Jerusalem’s final siege. The key verse symbolizes God’s judgment through the image of a boiling pot.

Chapter 25

Ezekiel 25:17
They shall know that I am the LORD

Why This Verse

Famous judgment formula

Chapter Insight

This chapter pronounces judgment on Israel’s neighboring nations. The key verse demonstrates that God holds all nations accountable for their actions.

Chapter 26

Ezekiel 26:14
Thou shalt be built no more

Why This Verse

Prophecy against Tyre

Chapter Insight

This chapter prophesies the fall of Tyre. The key verse reveals God’s sovereignty over powerful cities and empires.

Chapter 27

Ezekiel 27:3
O Tyre, thou hast said, I am of perfect beauty

Why This Verse

Beginning of Tyre’s lament

Chapter Insight

This chapter laments the downfall of Tyre’s commercial greatness. The key verse highlights the fragility of wealth and worldly success.

Chapter 28

Ezekiel 28:17
Thine heart was lifted up because of thy beauty

Why This Verse

Often cited regarding pride and downfall

Chapter Insight

This chapter condemns the pride of Tyre’s ruler. The key verse warns that self-exaltation leads to destruction.

Chapter 29

Ezekiel 29:3
I am against thee, Pharaoh

Why This Verse

Judgment on Egypt

Chapter Insight

This chapter announces judgment against Egypt. The key verse demonstrates the futility of trusting human power instead of God.

Chapter 30

Ezekiel 30:3
The day of the LORD is near

Why This Verse

Major prophetic theme

Chapter Insight

This chapter continues the prophecy against Egypt. The key verse emphasizes God’s authority over nations and rulers.

Chapter 31

Ezekiel 31:10
Because thou hast lifted up thyself in height

Why This Verse

Pride preceding judgment

Chapter Insight

This chapter compares Egypt to a great tree brought low. The key verse illustrates the downfall of pride before God.

Chapter 32

Ezekiel 32:7
I will cover the heavens and make the stars dark

Why This Verse

Cosmic judgment imagery

Chapter Insight

This chapter laments Pharaoh and Egypt’s coming judgment. The key verse reminds nations that earthly glory is temporary.

Chapter 33

Ezekiel 33:11
I have no pleasure in the death of the wicked

Why This Verse

One of Ezekiel’s central themes

Chapter Insight

This chapter renews Ezekiel’s commission as a watchman. The key verse emphasizes personal responsibility in responding to God’s warnings.

Chapter 34

Ezekiel 34:16
I will seek that which was lost

Why This Verse

Shepherd imagery later echoed by Jesus

Chapter Insight

This chapter condemns Israel’s false shepherds and promises God’s care. The key verse points to the coming Shepherd-King who will tend His flock.

Chapter 35

Ezekiel 35:15
As thou didst rejoice over the inheritance of Israel

Why This Verse

Judgment on Edom

Chapter Insight

This chapter prophesies judgment against Edom. The key verse highlights God’s justice toward those who oppose His people.

Chapter 36

Ezekiel 36:26
A new heart also will I give you

Why This Verse

One of the most important restoration promises

Chapter Insight

This chapter promises Israel’s restoration and spiritual renewal. The key verse foretells God giving His people a new heart and spirit.

Chapter 37

Ezekiel 37:5
I will cause breath to enter into you

Why This Verse

Famous valley of dry bones prophecy

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains the vision of the Valley of Dry Bones. The key verse demonstrates God’s power to bring life, restoration, and hope from apparent impossibility.

Chapter 38

Ezekiel 38:23
Thus will I magnify Myself

Why This Verse

God’s victory over Gog

Chapter Insight

This chapter introduces the prophecy concerning Gog. The key verse emphasizes God’s ultimate victory over hostile nations.

Chapter 39

Ezekiel 39:29
I have poured out My Spirit

Why This Verse

Restoration and renewal

Chapter Insight

This chapter continues God’s judgment against Gog. The key verse reveals God’s purpose of making His holiness known among the nations.

Chapter 40

Ezekiel 40:4
Declare all that thou seest

Why This Verse

Vision of the future temple

Chapter Insight

This chapter begins the vision of the future Temple. The key verse emphasizes God’s orderly and holy design for worship.

Chapter 41

Ezekiel 41:4
This is the most holy place

Why This Verse

Temple centerpiece

Chapter Insight

This chapter details the structure of the Temple. The key verse highlights the centrality of God’s dwelling place.

Chapter 42

Ezekiel 42:20
To make a separation between holy and common

Why This Verse

Temple holiness

Chapter Insight

This chapter describes the Temple chambers and sacred spaces. The key verse emphasizes holiness and separation unto God.

Chapter 43

Ezekiel 43:2
The glory of the God of Israel came

Why This Verse

Return of God’s glory

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the return of God’s glory to the Temple. The key verse marks the restoration of God’s presence among His people.

Chapter 44

Ezekiel 44:23
Teach My people the difference between holy and profane

Why This Verse

Priestly responsibility

Chapter Insight

This chapter establishes regulations for worship and priestly service. The key verse emphasizes reverence and holiness in approaching God.

Chapter 45

Ezekiel 45:9
Remove violence and spoil

Why This Verse

Call to justice

Chapter Insight

This chapter outlines sacred portions of land and worship practices. The key verse highlights justice and proper stewardship.

Chapter 46

Ezekiel 46:9
None shall return by the way he came

Why This Verse

Worship order

Chapter Insight

This chapter describes offerings and worship observances. The key verse emphasizes regular devotion and obedience.

Chapter 47

Ezekiel 47:9
Everything shall live where the river comes

Why This Verse

River of life vision

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains the vision of the river flowing from the Temple. The key verse portrays God’s life-giving blessing flowing outward to bring healing.

Chapter 48

Ezekiel 48:35
The LORD is there

Why This Verse

Final message of Ezekiel (Jehovah-Shammah)

Chapter Insight

This chapter concludes with the division of the land and the new city. The key verse declares the city’s name: “The LORD Is There.”
