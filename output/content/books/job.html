Job

Book Summary Insight

Job explores the mystery of suffering, the sovereignty of God, and the limits of human understanding. The book teaches that God’s wisdom and purposes transcend human comprehension and remain worthy of trust.

Chapter 1

Job 1:21
The LORD gave, and the LORD hath taken away

Why This Verse

Job’s response to loss

Chapter Insight

This chapter introduces Job’s righteousness and his sudden suffering. The key verse demonstrates unwavering worship despite devastating loss.

Chapter 2

Job 2:10
Shall we receive good and not evil?

Why This Verse

Job’s perseverance

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Job’s physical affliction and continued faithfulness. The key verse highlights perseverance when suffering intensifies.

Chapter 3

Job 3:3
Let the day perish wherein I was born

Why This Verse

Beginning of Job’s lament

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains Job’s lament over his suffering. The key verse expresses the depth of human anguish and confusion.

Chapter 4

Job 4:8
They that plow iniquity reap the same

Why This Verse

Eliphaz’s theology

Chapter Insight

This chapter begins Eliphaz’s first speech. The key verse reflects the mistaken belief that suffering is always the result of personal sin.

Chapter 5

Job 5:17
Happy is the man whom God correcteth

Why This Verse

Later quoted in Hebrews

Chapter Insight

This chapter continues Eliphaz’s counsel. The key verse emphasizes God’s discipline, though applied incorrectly to Job’s situation.

Chapter 6

Job 6:24
Teach me, and I will hold my tongue

Why This Verse

Job seeks understanding

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Job’s response to Eliphaz. The key verse reveals Job’s desire for understanding and compassion amid suffering.

Chapter 7

Job 7:17
What is man, that Thou shouldest magnify him?

Why This Verse

Famous reflection on humanity

Chapter Insight

This chapter expresses Job’s weariness and questions. The key verse highlights the brevity and difficulty of life.

Chapter 8

Job 8:7
Though thy beginning was small

Why This Verse

Frequently quoted encouragement

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains Bildad’s first speech. The key verse reflects confidence in traditional wisdom while misunderstanding Job’s circumstances.

Job 9:2
How should man be just with God?

Why This Verse

Central theological question

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Job’s recognition of God’s greatness. The key verse expresses humanity’s need for a mediator before God.

Job 10:12
Thou hast granted me life and favor

Why This Verse

Job recalls God’s care

Chapter Insight

This chapter continues Job’s plea for understanding. The key verse reveals his struggle to reconcile suffering with God’s purposes.

Chapter 11

Job 11:7
Canst thou by searching find out God?

Why This Verse

Zophar’s challenge

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains Zophar’s first speech. The key verse demonstrates the limitations of human understanding about God’s ways.

Job 12:13
With Him are wisdom and strength

Why This Verse

God’s sovereignty

Chapter Insight

This chapter begins Job’s response to his friends. The key verse affirms God’s sovereignty over all creation.

One of Job’s most quoted verses

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Job’s determination to trust God. The key verse expresses remarkable faith despite uncertainty.

Job 14:14

Hope beyond death

Chapter Insight

This chapter reflects on the frailty of human life. The key verse expresses hope beyond death.

Eliphaz’s accusation

Chapter Insight

Perseverance

Chapter Insight

Job 18:21
Such are the dwellings of the wicked

Job 19:25
I know that my Redeemer liveth

Why This Verse

Most famous verse in Job

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Job’s declaration of faith. The key verse expresses hope in a living Redeemer despite present suffering.

Why This Verse

Zophar’s argument

This chapter contains Zophar’s second speech. The key verse emphasizes the temporary nature of wicked prosperity.

Why This Verse

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains Bildad’s final remarks. The key verse emphasizes God’s holiness and human weakness.

He hangs the earth upon nothing

Chapter Insight

This chapter celebrates God’s majesty in creation. The key verse highlights the vastness of God’s power beyond human comprehension.

Job 27:5
Till I die I will not remove mine integrity

Why This Verse

Job’s resolve

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Job’s continued defense of his integrity. The key verse demonstrates his commitment to righteousness.

Chapter 28

Job 28:28

One of Job’s great themes

Chapter Insight

This chapter reflects on the nature of true wisdom. The key verse identifies reverence for God as the source of wisdom.

Chapter 29

Why This Verse

Job remembers former honor

This chapter recalls Job’s former blessings and influence. The key verse highlights the respect and honor he once enjoyed.

Chapter 30

Job 30:20

Job’s anguish

Chapter Insight

This chapter contrasts Job’s past honor with present suffering. The key verse expresses the depth of his humiliation.

I made a covenant with mine eyes

Why This Verse

Chapter Insight

This chapter presents Job’s final defense. The key verse demonstrates his commitment to personal integrity before God.

Chapter 32

Job 32:8

Elihu begins speaking

Chapter Insight

This chapter introduces Elihu. The key verse highlights the importance of speaking with humility and discernment.

Chapter 33

Job 33:4
The Spirit of God hath made me

Why This Verse

Creation and life

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains Elihu’s appeal to Job. The key verse emphasizes God’s desire to communicate and redeem.

Job 34:10

Why This Verse

Chapter Insight

This chapter defends God’s justice. The key verse affirms that God always acts righteously.

Chapter 35

Job 35:10

Why This Verse

Seeking God in suffering

Chapter Insight

This chapter discusses human righteousness and God’s transcendence. The key verse highlights God’s independence from human actions.

Job 36:26
God is great, and we know Him not

Why This Verse

Divine transcendence

Chapter Insight

This chapter continues Elihu’s defense of God’s ways. The key verse emphasizes God’s greatness and wisdom.

Job 37:14
Stand still and consider the wondrous works of God

Why This Verse

Preparation for God’s appearance

Chapter Insight

This chapter points to God’s majesty in nature. The key verse prepares the reader for God’s direct response.

Chapter 38

Job 38:4
Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth?

Chapter Insight

This chapter begins God’s answer from the whirlwind. The key verse demonstrates the limits of human understanding compared to God’s wisdom.

Chapter 39

Job 39:19
Hast thou given the horse strength?

Why This Verse

God’s wisdom in creation

Chapter Insight

This chapter continues God’s questions concerning creation. The key verse emphasizes God’s care and governance over all creatures.

Chapter 40

Job 40:4
Behold, I am vile; what shall I answer Thee?

Why This Verse

Job’s humility

Chapter Insight

This chapter calls Job to humility before God. The key verse highlights the inadequacy of challenging God’s wisdom.

Job 41:1
Canst thou draw out Leviathan?

Why This Verse

Chapter Insight

This chapter describes Leviathan. The key verse demonstrates God’s unmatched power over forces beyond human control.

Job 42:5
I have heard of Thee, but now mine eye seeth Thee

Why This Verse

Job’s final realization

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Job’s repentance, restoration, and vindication. The key verse reveals true wisdom through humility before God.
