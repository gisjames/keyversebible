John

Book Summary Insight

John presents Jesus as the eternal Son of God so that readers may believe in Him and receive eternal life. The book emphasizes faith, love, and the deity of Christ.

Chapter 1

John 1:1
In the beginning was the Word

Why This Verse

Foundational Christological verse

Chapter Insight

This chapter introduces Jesus as the eternal Word made flesh and records the calling of the first disciples. The key verse reveals Christ’s divine nature and His coming to dwell among humanity.

Chapter 2

John 2:5
Whatsoever He saith unto you, do it

Why This Verse

Cana miracle instruction

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Jesus’ first miracle and the cleansing of the Temple. The key verse demonstrates Jesus’ authority and reveals His glory.

Chapter 3

John 3:16
For God so loved the world

Why This Verse

Most famous verse in Christianity

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains Jesus’ conversation with Nicodemus. The key verse teaches that eternal life comes through faith in God’s Son.

Chapter 4

John 4:24
God is a Spirit

Why This Verse

Foundational statement on worship

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Jesus’ ministry to the Samaritan woman and many others. The key verse reveals Jesus as the source of living water and true worship.

Chapter 5

John 5:24
He that hears My word has everlasting life

Why This Verse

Assurance of salvation

Chapter Insight

This chapter recounts the healing at Bethesda and Jesus’ claims of equality with the Father. The key verse emphasizes Jesus’ authority to give life and execute judgment.

Chapter 6

John 6:35
I am the bread of life

Why This Verse

First major “I Am” statement

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the feeding of the five thousand and the Bread of Life discourse. The key verse presents Jesus as the true bread from heaven who satisfies spiritual hunger.

Chapter 7

John 7:38
Rivers of living water

Why This Verse

Holy Spirit imagery

Chapter Insight

This chapter records growing debate concerning Jesus’ identity. The key verse invites all who thirst to come to Christ and receive living water.

Chapter 8

John 8:32
Ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free

Why This Verse

One of the most quoted verses in the NT

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Jesus’ teaching about truth, freedom, and His divine identity. The key verse reveals that true freedom comes through abiding in Christ and His truth.

Chapter 9

John 9:25
Whereas I was blind, now I see

Why This Verse

Testimony of transformation

Chapter Insight

This chapter recounts the healing of a man born blind. The key verse demonstrates that Jesus is the Light of the World who brings both physical and spiritual sight.

Chapter 10

John 10:10
I am come that they might have life

Why This Verse

Abundant life verse

Chapter Insight

This chapter presents Jesus as the Good Shepherd. The key verse emphasizes Christ’s sacrificial care for His sheep and His authority to give eternal life.

Chapter 11

John 11:25
I am the resurrection and the life

Why This Verse

One of Jesus’ greatest declarations

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the raising of Lazarus. The key verse declares Jesus to be the resurrection and the life.

Chapter 12

John 12:32
If I be lifted up from the earth

Why This Verse

Crucifixion and salvation

Chapter Insight

This chapter marks the final public phase of Jesus’ ministry. The key verse emphasizes faith in Christ as the means of receiving eternal life.

Chapter 13

John 13:35
By this shall all men know that ye are My disciples

Why This Verse

Love as Christian witness

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Jesus washing the disciples’ feet and giving a new commandment. The key verse teaches that love and humble service identify Christ’s followers.

Chapter 14

John 14:6
I am the way, the truth, and the life

Why This Verse

One of the most cited verses in the Bible

Chapter Insight

This chapter begins the Upper Room Discourse. The key verse reveals Jesus as the exclusive way to the Father and promises the coming of the Holy Spirit.

Chapter 15

John 15:5
I am the vine, ye are the branches

Why This Verse

Abiding in Christ

Chapter Insight

This chapter presents Jesus as the True Vine. The key verse emphasizes that spiritual fruitfulness comes only through abiding in Christ.

Chapter 16

John 16:33
In the world ye shall have tribulation

Why This Verse

Peace through Christ

Chapter Insight

This chapter prepares the disciples for Jesus’ departure and the coming of the Holy Spirit. The key verse promises that sorrow will ultimately be transformed into joy through Christ’s victory.

Chapter 17

John 17:17
Sanctify them through Thy truth

Why This Verse

High Priestly Prayer

Chapter Insight

This chapter contains Jesus’ High Priestly Prayer. The key verse highlights Jesus’ desire for unity, sanctification, and eternal fellowship for His followers.

Chapter 18

John 18:37
For this cause came I into the world, that I should bear witness unto the truth

Why This Verse

Jesus before Pilate

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Jesus’ arrest, trial, and Peter’s denial. The key verse demonstrates Christ’s willing submission to the Father’s redemptive plan.

Chapter 19

John 19:30
It is finished

Why This Verse

One of Christ’s final sayings

Chapter Insight

This chapter recounts Jesus’ crucifixion, death, and burial. The key verse declares the completion of Christ’s atoning work.

Chapter 20

John 20:29
Blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed

Why This Verse

Faith without sight

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the resurrection appearances of Jesus. The key verse reveals the purpose of John’s Gospel: that people may believe and have life through Christ.

Chapter 21

John 21:17
Lord, Thou knowest that I love Thee

Why This Verse

Restoration of Peter

Chapter Insight

This chapter records Jesus’ appearance by the Sea of Galilee and the restoration of Peter. The key verse emphasizes love for Christ expressed through faithful service.
