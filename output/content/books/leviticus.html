Leviticus

Book Summary Insight

Leviticus teaches how a holy God can dwell among His covenant people through sacrifice, priesthood, worship, and holy living. The recurring theme is found throughout the book: “Be holy, for I am holy.” (Leviticus 11:44; 19:2; 20:26).

Chapter 1

Leviticus 1:3
Burnt offering without blemish

Why This Verse

Defines acceptable sacrifice

Chapter Insight

This chapter introduces the burnt offering as an act of complete dedication to God. The key verse emphasizes worship through voluntary sacrifice and atonement.

Chapter 2

Leviticus 2:13
Salt of the covenant

Why This Verse

Frequently cited covenant symbol

Chapter Insight

This chapter describes the grain offering as an expression of gratitude and devotion. The key verse highlights offering the best of one’s labor to God.

Chapter 3

Leviticus 3:17
Neither fat nor blood

Why This Verse

Permanent ordinance emphasis

Chapter Insight

This chapter explains the fellowship (peace) offering. The key verse emphasizes reconciliation and communion with God through sacrifice.

Chapter 4

Leviticus 4:35
Atonement shall be made

Why This Verse

Central sin-offering theme

Chapter Insight

This chapter establishes procedures for sin offerings. The key verse demonstrates God’s provision for dealing with unintentional sin.

Chapter 5

Leviticus 5:5
Confess the sin committed

Why This Verse

Foundational confession text

Chapter Insight

This chapter continues instructions concerning sin and guilt offerings. The key verse stresses confession and accountability before God.

Chapter 6

Leviticus 6:13
Fire shall never go out

Why This Verse

One of Leviticus’ most quoted verses

Chapter Insight

This chapter provides further regulations for offerings and priestly duties. The key verse emphasizes continual devotion symbolized by the ever-burning fire.

Chapter 7

Leviticus 7:37
Law of the offerings

Why This Verse

Summary of sacrificial system

Chapter Insight

This chapter concludes the laws regarding sacrifices. The key verse underscores the holiness of worship and proper participation in sacred offerings.

Chapter 8

Leviticus 8:33
Seven days of consecration

Why This Verse

Priesthood inauguration emphasis

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the consecration of Aaron and his sons as priests. The key verse highlights obedience to God’s appointed process for ministry.

Chapter 9

Leviticus 9:24
Fire came from before the LORD

Why This Verse

Divine acceptance of worship

Chapter Insight

This chapter describes the beginning of priestly service and God’s acceptance of the offerings. The key verse marks God’s visible approval through His glory.

Chapter 10

Leviticus 10:3
I will be sanctified in them that come near Me

Why This Verse

Holiness principle

Chapter Insight

This chapter records the judgment of Nadab and Abihu for unauthorized worship. The key verse teaches the seriousness of approaching God with reverence.

Chapter 11

Leviticus 11:44
Be holy, for I am holy

Why This Verse

Major biblical holiness text

Chapter Insight

This chapter establishes dietary laws distinguishing clean and unclean animals. The key verse connects holiness with daily living and obedience.

Chapter 12

Leviticus 12:8
Provision for the poor

Why This Verse

Frequently noted sacrificial exception

Chapter Insight

This chapter provides purification regulations after childbirth. The key verse emphasizes ceremonial cleansing and restoration to worship.

Chapter 13

Leviticus 13:45
Unclean person isolated

Why This Verse

Most recognized leprosy regulation

Chapter Insight

This chapter outlines laws concerning skin diseases and ceremonial uncleanness. The key verse highlights the need to identify and address impurity.

Chapter 14

Leviticus 14:57
To teach when it is clean or unclean

Why This Verse

Purpose statement

Chapter Insight

This chapter explains the cleansing of those healed from leprosy. The key verse demonstrates God’s provision for restoration and reintegration.

Chapter 15

Leviticus 15:31
Separate from uncleanness

Why This Verse

Summary principle

Chapter Insight

This chapter addresses bodily discharges and ceremonial purity. The key verse reinforces the importance of holiness before God.

Chapter 16

Leviticus 16:30
On this day atonement shall be made

Why This Verse

Central Day of Atonement verse

Chapter Insight

This chapter establishes the Day of Atonement. The key verse presents the central annual act of atonement for Israel’s sins.

Chapter 17

Leviticus 17:11
Life of the flesh is in the blood

Why This Verse

Foundational atonement theology

Chapter Insight

This chapter regulates sacrifices and the sanctity of blood. The key verse teaches that life is in the blood and that atonement comes through sacrifice.

Chapter 18

Leviticus 18:22
Thou shalt not lie with mankind

Why This Verse

Most cited verse in chapter

Chapter Insight

This chapter prohibits immoral sexual practices. The key verse calls God’s people to distinct holiness from surrounding nations.

Chapter 19

Leviticus 19:18
Love thy neighbor as thyself

Why This Verse

One of the most quoted verses in the Bible

Chapter Insight

This chapter applies holiness to everyday relationships and conduct. The key verse summarizes the chapter’s ethical teaching through love of neighbor.

Chapter 20

Leviticus 20:26
Be holy unto Me

Why This Verse

Covenant holiness

Chapter Insight

This chapter outlines penalties for serious sins and idolatry. The key verse emphasizes God’s call for His people to be holy.

Chapter 21

Leviticus 21:8
He shall be holy unto thee

Why This Verse

Priestly holiness

Chapter Insight

This chapter sets standards for priestly holiness. The key verse highlights the priest’s unique responsibility to represent God.

Chapter 22

Leviticus 22:31
Keep My commandments and do them

Why This Verse

Obedience emphasis

Chapter Insight

This chapter regulates the handling of holy offerings. The key verse stresses reverence for God’s holy name.

Chapter 23

Leviticus 23:3
Sabbath of solemn rest

Why This Verse

Foundation of sacred calendar

Chapter Insight

This chapter establishes Israel’s sacred feasts and appointed times. The key verse emphasizes worship through remembrance of God’s works.

Chapter 24

Leviticus 24:22
One law for stranger and native

Why This Verse

Equality before the law

Chapter Insight

This chapter addresses worship practices and justice within the community. The key verse presents the principle of equal justice and accountability.

Chapter 25

Leviticus 25:10
Proclaim liberty throughout the land

Why This Verse

Famous Jubilee verse (on the Liberty Bell)

Chapter Insight

This chapter introduces the Sabbath Year and Year of Jubilee. The key verse teaches that the land and people ultimately belong to God.

Chapter 26

Leviticus 26:12
I will walk among you and be your God

Why This Verse

Covenant blessing climax

Chapter Insight

This chapter presents blessings for obedience and consequences for disobedience. The key verse expresses God’s desire to dwell among His people.

Chapter 27

Leviticus 27:30
The tithe is the LORD’s

Why This Verse

Most cited verse on tithing in the chapter

Chapter Insight

This chapter concludes Leviticus with laws concerning vows and dedications. The key verse emphasizes honoring commitments made to God.
