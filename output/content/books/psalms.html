Psalms

Book Summary Insight

Psalms is the Bible’s hymnbook, expressing worship, lament, thanksgiving, repentance, and praise. The book teaches that every human experience can be brought honestly before God in faith.

Chapter 1

Psalm 1:1
Blessed is the man

Why This Verse

Opening wisdom psalm

Chapter Insight

This psalm contrasts the way of the righteous and the wicked. The key verse emphasizes that true blessing comes from delighting in God’s Word.
