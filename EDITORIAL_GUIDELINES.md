# Editorial Guidelines

## Editorial Philosophy

The guide identifies one representative verse for each chapter, not the only important verse.

## Book Summary Insight

- One concise paragraph.
- Introduce the book's purpose and major themes.
- Prefer clear language over technical terminology.

## Why This Verse

- Explain the editorial reason for the selection.
- Remain concise and avoid simply repeating the verse.

## Chapter Insight

- Summarize the chapter as a whole.
- Explain how the key verse fits the chapter's literary and theological context.
- Use the present tense and a neutral devotional tone.

## Formatting

- Use complete book names.
- Format references as `Genesis 1:1`.
- Use the standard headings: Key Verse, Short Identifier, Why This Verse, Chapter Insight, and Book Summary Insight.

## Attribution

Artificial intelligence may assist with research and drafting. Final editorial judgment and publication decisions remain with the compiler and editor.
